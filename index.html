What counts as Owner Total Compensation (CCPC):
· T4 Salary paid to the owner (deductible corporate expense)
· Payroll taxes — employer CPP contributions & EI premiums
· Health, Dental & Vision Insurance premiums
· Vehicle — lease, payments, insurance & fuel
· Retirement contributions (RRSP, Group RRSP, DPSP)
· Life / Disability Insurance premiums
· Cell phone & personal technology devices
· Club memberships, travel, meals & other perks

Do not include dividends — they are paid from after-tax corporate profit and are reflected in Net Income.
