Methodology: This blended valuation equally weights (⅓ each) three methods: the SDE method (2.0× — standard for profitable MEP contractors with strong owner involvement), the Revenue method (0.5× — benchmarks against industry revenue norms), and the Net Income method (5.0× — a P/E-style earnings multiple reflecting true bottom-line profitability). Final value is the simple average of all three. Actual market value varies based on customer concentration, backlog, technician bench strength, geographic market, and current M&A conditions.
